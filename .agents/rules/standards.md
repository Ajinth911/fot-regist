# Project Design & Quality Standards

Strict guidelines that must be adhered to across all design, UI/UX, copy, and code decisions:

## 1. Aesthetic & UI Rules (Anti-Vibe-Product)
- **Colors & Gradients**: Strictly no purple gradients or generic SaaS neon gradients.
- **Buttons**: Strictly no pill-shaped buttons (use clean, deliberate, sharp/subtly-rounded geometric corners instead).
- **Icons**: Strictly no emoji icons as UI elements. Use professional vector/SVG iconography (e.g. Lucide, Heroicons).
- **Animations**: Strictly no over-the-top scroll animations, parallax bloat, or custom cursor animations. Keep interactions snappy, performant, and purposeful.

## 2. Content & Copywriting
- **Hero Sections**: Clear, specific, value-driven text. Strictly no vague buzzwords or empty marketing puffery.
- **Punctuation**: Strictly no em dashes (`—`). Use crisp, well-structured sentences.
- **Authenticity**:
  - Strictly no fake reviews, testimonials, or social proof.
  - Strictly no fake counters, fabricated metrics, or artificial urgency tickers.
  - Strictly no AI-slop copy or synthetic placeholder fluff.
- **Imagery**: Real, high-fidelity, contextual assets only. Strictly no generic AI-slop illustrations or uncanny generated stock photos.

## 3. Pre-Launch Checklist (Blocking Requirements)
No launch or production deployment until all of the following are complete:
- [ ] Connect and verify custom domain.
- [ ] Ensure valid, crisp favicon is configured and linked.
- [ ] Strip any "Made with AI" tags, badges, or watermarks.
- [ ] Dedicated Privacy Policy page created and linked.
- [ ] Dedicated Terms & Conditions page created and linked.
